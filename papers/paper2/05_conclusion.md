## 5.1 Conclusions

We set out to answer a question the SCRES literature asks but does not measure: **how much of the
value attributed to "closing the learning loop" in a discrete-event resilience model is actually
there to be captured, and which component of a learning system captures it?** Our answer separates
into four measured statements.

**First, the opportunity is physical and mechanism-specific.** In the two-product extension a
clairvoyant policy beats the best of all 4⁸ = 65,536 open-loop production calendars by
`H_PI = 0.15151` (simultaneous safe LCB95 `0.11562`), and the same oracle recovers **exactly zero**
when the two products are made fungible
(`results/program_o/full_des_hpi_translation_v1/validation_custody_verdict_v1.json`, via
`papers/paper2/results_table.md`). The ceiling is a property of non-fungible contention over a
shared assembly line, not of the environment's noise.

**Second, the instrument discriminates.** The same measurement apparatus applied to the
thesis-native risk envelope — 45 risk profiles × 18 constant postures × 6 development seeds,
4,860 evaluations — returns a maximum profile headroom of `0.00024068` raw / `6.93 × 10⁻⁵` safe and
**zero passing doors** (`results/garrido_risk_headroom_sensitivity_v1/result.json`, fields
`group_budget_summaries[*].H_profile_raw`, `passing_doors`, `n_evaluations`). A protocol that
reports 0.15 in one setting and 0.0002 in another is measuring something; the null is not the
instrument's default output.

**Third, the value is in the feedback, not in the function approximator.** Ten independently
seeded recurrent policies beat the complete enumerated open-loop frontier in 3/3 cells
(`H_OL` LCB95 `+0.043 / +0.037 / +0.066`), while showing no incremental value against the strongest
frozen state-rich classical controller (`Δ_N` point `−0.0017 / −0.0027 / −0.0015`, LCB95
`−0.009 / −0.014 / −0.008`; 1/0/2 of 10 seeds beating both comparators). Closing the loop is worth
a lot; making the loop neural is worth, on this evidence, nothing measurable.

**Fourth, the aggregate objective buys the mean at the cost of the minimum.** The learner matches
the classical controller almost exactly on aggregate endpoints while degrading
`max_backlog_age` (+122.7), `service_loss_auc` (+908k) and `unresolved_orders` (+0.20)
(`SECOND_OPINION_CLAUDE.md:33-37`). This is a mean↔minimum substitution, and it is invisible to
every scalarised resilience index in the field — including the thesis's own `ReT` (Eq. 5.5),
Garrido's `R` (2024, Eq. 6), Ding's `R̂` (2026, Eq. 55) and Kong's composite score (2026) — because
none of them carries a per-product term that could fail.

The methodological consequence is the paper's primary contribution: **a measured ceiling, an
exhaustively enumerated comparator frontier, an equivalence test with a declared indifference zone
and declared power, and an equity guardrail that is allowed to fail.** Each of the four is a way the
protocol can return a verdict we did not want, and three of them did.

**Status of the evidence.** Levels 3–4 now rest on **two independent tape sets**: the calibration
ladder above, and Program Q's preregistered replication on N = 256 virgin seeds per cell (block
`7490001–7490256`, executed 2026-07-18), custodied bit-exactly in frozen commit `031d0af`
(`results/program_q/confirmation_v1_20260718/artifacts/confirmation/`; `evaluation/result.json`
SHA-256 `62f6fd39…` ✓, `adjudication.json` SHA-256 `e13e17f0…` ✓). E1 replicated H_OL superiority
in 3/3 cells (LCB95 +0.066/+0.062/+0.106); Δ_N equivalence held with every CI95 inside ±0.01;
the per-product equity guardrail failed in 3/3 cells, so the preregistered composite terminated as
`STOP_Q_NO_REPLICATED_LEARNED_ADAPTATION` (`adjudication.json::verdict`) — a compound-gate
outcome that does not unsettle either endpoint (§3.5). The evidential status of Q — executed,
adjudicated, and not to be re-adjudicated or re-seeded — is fixed by the dated PI decision
`docs/PROGRAM_Q_CANONICAL_EVIDENCE_STATUS_2026-08-25.md`, which supersedes any earlier internal
note describing Q as pending or unopened.

## 5.2 Endpoint identification, and the sign-inversion sensitivity we publish ourselves

The `ReT` construct is not one endpoint but a family, and the members do not agree. We treat this
as a finding about the family rather than as a defect to be hidden, and we state our identification
decision before showing the evidence.

**The situation, stated before the decision.** Two of our own frozen contracts already name
*different* primaries, on different tracks:

- Program O / O-R / Q — the contracts that produce every Level 1–4 number in this paper — fix
  `ret_excel_request_snapshot_v2`. Its defining property is the *provenance and timing* of `Bt`/`Ut`
  (frozen on each order at request generation `OPTj`, never reconstructed from completion time), and
  the per-order formula is explicitly **not clipped**
  (`docs/RET_EXCEL_REQUEST_SNAPSHOT_V2_CONTRACT_2026-07-14.md:19-21`).
- The thesis-native risk-corrective track fixes `ret_excel_clipped_0_1`
  (`contracts/ret_metric_repair_confirmation_v1.json::primary_endpoint`).

**Our decision (declared as ours, not the thesis's).** The manuscript's primary endpoint is
`ret_excel_request_snapshot_v2`. The reason is not that it is the most stable member — the evidence
below says it is not the axis on which stability turns — but that it is the endpoint under which
every reported estimand was computed, and switching now would require re-scoring the entire ladder
rather than relabelling it. `ret_excel_full_ledger` is reported as a **declared, pre-specified
sensitivity**, never as an alternative primary. The thesis does not settle the choice; we settle it,
and the price of settling it is the table below.

**The test.** Holding the policy pair, the comparator and the physics fixed, we re-score the same
frozen incumbent against the same MPC arm on two **disjoint** tape blocks — the corrective
confirmation block (roots 1810001–1810016, n = 16) and the Step-3 pooled block
(roots 1422001–1423006, n = 12) — across the seven-member endpoint family. No new simulation and no
new seeds: this is a re-aggregation of frozen artifacts
(`results/metric_audit/ret_metric_repair_confirmation_v1/result.json`,
`contracts/ret_metric_repair_confirmation_v1.json`, `results/step3_*/full/rows.json`).

*(Block A intervals are the frozen artifact's own CI95; Block B intervals are paired percentile

**Three readings, in increasing order of how little they assume.**

1. *Cross-block (assumes the blocks are exchangeable).* Six of seven endpoints invert sign for R2r.
   Because `delivered_rations` — a physical count, not a metric convention — inverts too, part of
   this is a genuine difference in what the MPC arm did on the two blocks, not only a scoring
   artifact. We therefore do **not** claim the two blocks are exchangeable.

2. *Within-block, and this is the reading that survives (assumes nothing about block
   comparability).* On the **same tapes**, with the **same policy pair**, `ret_excel_full_ledger`
   disagrees in sign with **all four** other members of the family — `ret_excel`,
   `ret_excel_clipped_0_1`, `ret_excel_quantity_time_clipped_0_1` and `ret_thesis` — in **both**
   blocks: +0.0125 vs −0.0045 in Block A, and −0.0122 vs +0.0084 in Block B. The `ReT` family
   therefore does not split on clipping, which is what one would guess from the member names; it
   splits on **ledger provenance**, with the full-ledger member standing alone against the four
   snapshot/visible-ledger members. No block-comparability assumption is needed to see it, and it
   cannot be attributed to sampling: every one of these four intervals excludes zero.

3. *Family-conditional (the part that is easy to miss).* R1r shows **no** disagreement, within or
   across blocks, on any endpoint. The instability is not a property of the metric in the abstract;
   it appears where the arm actually trades delivered volume against per-order lateness, and
   vanishes where it does not. An endpoint-robustness claim must therefore be made per operating
   regime, not once for the model.

**Why the mechanism is the same one as §5.1's fourth conclusion.** In Block B the MPC arm delivers
**+42,784 more rations** and simultaneously scores **worse** on the clipped endpoint; in Block A it
delivers **25,399 fewer** and scores **better**. The clipped and full-ledger members weight
unfulfilled-versus-late differently, so they price the volume/lateness trade in opposite directions.
This is the same mean↔minimum substitution that defeats the per-product guardrail — observed here in
the *endpoint*, where §5.1 observed it in the *policy*. A scalarised resilience index is not merely
unable to detect the substitution; under a different admissible scalarisation of the same construct
it can *reward* it.

**The incumbent is the third axis, and we can show it directly.** A companion analysis
(`results/paper_prep/endpoint_sensitivity.json`) scores the same MPC arm on the same Step-3 block
against the *pooled best static posture* — `[0, 672, 168]` for R2r
(`results/step3_pooled/result.json::families.R2r.best_static_posture`) — rather than against the
frozen incumbent `[336, 0, 168]`. Where the two analyses share an estimand they agree to five
decimals (full-ledger against best-static: −0.00099085 there, −0.0009908 here). Where the incumbent
differs, the R2r contrasts move by an order of magnitude and several change sign. Endpoint, incumbent
and block are three axes, not one, and a result is only well posed once all three are named.

**What we conclude, and what we do not.** We conclude that no claim of the form "controller X
converts headroom" is well posed in this model family until **endpoint, incumbent and tape block are
all three fixed and declared**; we fix ours above. We do **not** conclude that our Level 1–4 results
are endpoint-fragile: those are computed on the two-product Program O physics under a single frozen
endpoint with the comparator reselected inside every resample, and the R2r instability documented
here belongs to the thesis-native risk-corrective track, a different physics and a different
comparator set. That distinction is itself a claim a reviewer may test, which is why the artifact,
the script and both blocks ship with the paper.

## 5.3 Limitations

**The environment is a researcher-defined mechanism study.** The two-product extension is motivated
by the thesis's documented aggregation of 21 ration classes into a single product, but it is not a
calibrated model of the real portfolio. The `(ρ, s)` cells are an experimental envelope we chose;
the 24-hour commitment latency, the fixed-clock freight semantics and the eight-decision horizon are
**our** assumptions, declared as such. Every quantitative claim about `H_PI`, `H_OL` and `Δ_N` is a
claim about this environment.

**The thesis-native envelope has almost no headroom, and we know three reasons why.** The assembly
line runs at a 2.6 % capacity margin at `S = 1` (17,948 vs ≈17,500 rations/week; `docs/PRESENTACION_GARRIDO_2026-03-25.md:123`), so there is no
allocation decision to make; the highest-weight ReT branch (autotomy, weight 1.0) is structurally
unreachable because `GARRIDO_FULFILLMENT_DELAY_HOURS = 54.0` exceeds `LT = 48`
(`supply_chain/config.py:146`); and at `op12` an uninformed placebo beats the state-conditioned
rule. These are properties of the reproduced thesis model, and they bound how much of our positive
result can be read back onto it.

**No deployment-grade safety claim.** The preregistered joint tail gate (CVaR10 non-inferiority at
zero margin) was not met in two cells. An instrument audit showed that a zero-margin lower-bound
requirement is arithmetically a superiority test whose 80 %-power threshold at this sample size is a
true tail improvement of ≈ +0.079 — larger than the mean effect itself. The gate was stringent by
design, not defective; but it cannot distinguish "non-inferior" from "not superior", so tail
behaviour is reported descriptively and nothing is claimed about deployment safety.

**The comparator is not deployable.** The strongest classical controller plans with the correct
decision model. Equivalence to an oracle-model controller is a weaker managerial statement than
equivalence to something a firm could run, and the reviewer objection is fair. A preregistered
secondary arm — MPC on an *estimated* model, or restricted to the learner's partial observation —
is specified and unexecuted. Until it runs, the honest scope is: *the learner matches a controller
that knows the model.*

**Selection hygiene is not yet fully closed.** The Gate 0 design selected and evaluated on the same
tapes; the split-tape correction (`research/literature_context/reports/AUDITORIA_GATE0_SPLIT_TAPES.md`) is written and not executed, and
the winner's-curse bias delta is therefore unreported. We flag this rather than absorb it.

**Reproducibility caveats we would rather state than have found.** Two integrity anchors were
re-frozen on 2026-08-24 with documented cause (`docs/CUSTODIA_ANCHORS_RECONGELADO_2026-08-24.md`);
one of them was demonstrably frozen incorrectly at its origin commit — the test fails at the very
commit that wrote the golden, with unchanged physics files. Separately, the war-GSA overlay manifest
reconstructs to within 1–2 ulp (worst relative difference 2.1 × 10⁻¹⁶) rather than bit-exactly. Both
are documented; neither changes a reported number; neither is presented as bit-exactness.

**One description of the frozen arms stands, by contract.** An earlier internal review described
belief-MPC as the frozen primary arm;
`contracts/program_o_ret_only_learner_v1.json` settles it otherwise: the primary learner arm is
**RecurrentPPO** (LSTM 64, MLP [64,64], 200,192 steps), with belief-MPC inside the ten-classical
comparator family — whose per-cell winners in the replication were `min_cost_flow__2`,
`min_cost_flow__2`, `max_pressure__0`. Every section of this manuscript names the arms that way;
the abstract is written from this description.

## 5.4 External validity

**Within the modelled system.** The results transfer to settings that share the mechanism we
isolated: two or more non-fungible outputs contending for one non-substitutable bottleneck, with a
commitment lag between decision and effect, and a demand mix that switches regime. Where the
mechanism is absent — where the resource is fungible — we measured the headroom to be exactly zero,
so we predict no value from closed-loop control there, and that prediction is falsifiable with one
run of the fungibility switch on any candidate system.

**To military food logistics generally.** Limited, and limited by something outside our control. The
scope questions RT1–RT5 posed to the source author (whether the `current`/`increased` risk
frequency and impact scenarios are defensible MFSC configurations, the units and authority
semantics, and the meaning of `sumBt` in the source ledger) remain
`UNANSWERED_CLAIM_SCOPE_ONLY`. Until they are answered, the risk-scenario envelope is a
**researcher stress extension**, not a validated operating range, and we label it so in every table.
The fidelity we do claim is narrow and measured: exact reproduction of the resilience formula over
47,546 workbook cells with 0 mismatches, and −4.43 % on ECS against the thesis's Chapter 6.
Behavioural reproduction under endogenous calendar generation is *not* claimed (mean gap 0.1194).

**To the SCRES-RL literature.** The protocol transfers further than the result does, and that is the
intended contribution. The four elements — a perfect-information ceiling computed before training
with a mechanism placebo that must return exactly zero; a comparator defined as the maximum over an
enumerated action frontier, reselected inside every resample; an indifference zone with declared
power so that "no difference" is an estimand rather than a failure to reject; and a per-product
equity guardrail permitted to fail — require only that the action space be enumerable or bounded and
that a clairvoyant policy be computable offline. Neither condition is special to this model. Where
the action space is too large to enumerate, the frontier maximum degrades gracefully into a
best-of-K with K declared.

**Where it does not transfer.** Our comparator frontier is exhaustive because the decision is
4⁸-dimensional; network-reconfiguration settings with combinatorial action spaces (Ding 2026) cannot
enumerate, and there the ceiling must come from a relaxation bound rather than from clairvoyant
enumeration — a substitution that weakens the guarantee and should be declared, not glossed. Our
single-agent setting also says nothing about credit assignment across cooperating agents.

**Three predictions this paper makes that a later study can falsify.** (i) Making the contended
resource fungible drives the measured ceiling to zero in any system exhibiting our mechanism.
(ii) In any environment where a state-rich classical controller with the correct model is
available, a generic recurrent learner will be equivalent to it within a declared indifference zone
rather than superior. (iii) Any learned policy optimising an aggregate resilience scalar under
contention will fail a per-product non-inferiority guardrail — and the paper that reports no such
failure has not measured one.

## 5.5 What would change our conclusions

We state the falsifiers so they are on the record; the replication has since run, and each row
records where it stands.

| Result | What would overturn it |
|---|---|
| Ceiling is mechanism-specific | A non-zero safe `H_PI` under the fungibility switch |
| No neural premium against the ten classical configurations evaluated | `LCB95(Δ_N) ≥ +0.01` in 3/3 cells on virgin tapes with the policies frozen by SHA — Program Q opened those tapes and the premium arm failed in 3/3 (`adjudication.json::cell_gates.<cell>.neural_premium`) |
| Feedback carries the value | The complete open-loop frontier matching the learner once reselected inside every resample — not met: H_OL replicated positive in 3/3 cells on virgin tapes |
| Equity failure is mechanistic | Per-product non-inferiority passing at equal aggregate fill and equal scheduled resources — the guardrail failed again in all three replication cells |
| Endpoint split is real | A tie-semantics or ledger-timing convention under which clipped and full-ledger members agree in sign on both blocks |

The corresponding negative results are already in the record, dated and committed, including the two
occasions on which development-tape optimism died on sealed tapes.
