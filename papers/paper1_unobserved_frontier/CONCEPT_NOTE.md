# Measurement Before Learning — concept note

Paper 1 is retained as a concept note while Paper 2 is prepared for C&IE. Its scope is measurement
integrity, not a second concurrent C&IE submission.

The note develops three linked propositions:

1. population closure: rankings can change when abandonment and omitted claimants change the measured
   population;
2. estimability under censoring: recovery summaries require an explicit endpoint when runs terminate
   before recovery;
3. evaluation-mesh coherence: changing cadence or evaluation frequency can change the estimand,
   not merely improve measurement precision.

Cobb–Douglas is used only where it passes the concrete abandonment falsifier; it does not “repair
ReT”. TTR is a distinct estimand and is reported with (P(TTR\le\tau)), restricted mean and absorbed
fraction. Non-anticipative normalization travels with Paper 2 as an integrity control.
