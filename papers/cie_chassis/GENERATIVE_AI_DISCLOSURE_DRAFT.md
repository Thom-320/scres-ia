# Generative-AI disclosure — author-confirmation draft

The current C&IE guide requires a separate declaration before the references
when generative AI materially supported manuscript preparation. The following
statement records the tools known to have influenced editorial organization,
drafting, or review. Every author must confirm that the list is complete and
approve the final wording.

> During the preparation of this work, the authors used OpenAI Codex and
> Anthropic Claude to support manuscript organization, editorial drafting,
> clarity, and consistency checks. The authors reviewed, verified, and edited
> all AI-assisted output and take full responsibility for the content of the
> publication. No generative-AI-produced scientific result, table, or figure is
> included.

This statement is included in the anonymized manuscript because it contains no
author identity. Tool use for scientific computation remains governed by the
methods, code, frozen artifacts, and reproducibility record rather than this
writing disclosure.
